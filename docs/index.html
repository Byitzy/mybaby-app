MyBaby

A simple iPhone app for tracking your newborn's feeds, diapers, sleep and health, made for one-handed use at 3 a.m. With a diary for the moments you want to remember, monthly summaries, and letters for your baby to open when they're older.

Log hands-free with Siri: “Log a wet diaper in MyBaby”, “MyBaby, the baby's asleep” or “Ask MyBaby when the baby last ate”.

Why we made MyBaby

We made MyBaby as new parents. In those first weeks we wanted to really understand what our baby needed: when they last ate, how they were sleeping, whether we were changing enough diapers, and what to tell the pediatrician. We also wanted to take turns at night without one of us running on empty.

So we built the app we wished we had, with one rule: a baby's information belongs to their parents, and nobody else.

  • Your data stays with you. Everything is kept on your iPhone and, if you choose, in your own iCloud, end-to-end encrypted. No MyBaby servers, accounts, ads, tracking or analytics. We never see your data.
  • No AI. There are no AI tools in MyBaby: no chatbot, no AI suggestions, and your baby's log is never analyzed or used to train anything. The one exception is cry alerts, which use Apple's built-in sound recognition to tell crying from other sounds, entirely on your iPhone.
  • Always free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no premium tier, not even donations.
  • Honest feedback is all we ask. Tell us what works and how we can make MyBaby better: in the app under Settings › Send feedback, or at mybaby@manhattin.com.
  • Open for transparency. The app's code is public, so anyone can check these promises for themselves.

MyBaby is a tracking tool, not medical advice.
