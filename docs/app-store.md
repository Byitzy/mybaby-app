## Description (4,000)

We made MyBaby as new parents. In those first weeks we wanted to really understand what our baby needed:
when they last ate, how they were sleeping, whether we were changing enough diapers, and what to tell
the pediatrician. We also wanted to take turns at night without one of us running on empty.

So we built the app we wished we had, with one rule: our baby's information belongs to us, and nobody else.

YOUR BABY'S DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• Everything is kept on your iPhone, and, if you choose, in your own iCloud, end-to-end encrypted.
• There are no MyBaby servers, no accounts and no sign-up.
• No ads, no tracking and no analytics. We never see your data.
• No AI. There are no AI tools in MyBaby: no chatbot, no AI suggestions, and your baby's log is never
  analyzed or used to train anything. The one exception is cry alerts, which use Apple's built-in sound
  recognition, entirely on your iPhone.
• Our code is public, so anyone can check that this is true.

ALWAYS FREE
We won't ask for money: no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no premium tier, not even donations.
All we ask for is honest feedback. Tell us what works, what doesn't, and how we can make MyBaby better,
right from Settings › Send feedback.

TRACK THE DAY IN A TAP OR TWO
• Feeds: breastfeeding with a left/right timer, bottles of breast milk or formula, and solids
• Diapers, sleep, baths, pumping and the milk stash, temperature and medicine
• Big buttons and a dim 3 AM mode, made for one-handed use in the middle of the night
• Undo on every quick log

SHARE THE NIGHT
• Invite your partner and share one log through iCloud
• Night shifts split the night fairly, with gentle vibrating alarms for whoever's on duty
• Cry alerts buzz only the parent on duty; use a spare iPad or iPhone as a nursery monitor with white noise
• Smart reminders learn your baby's rhythm from your own log

HEALTH AND CHECKUPS
• Appointments with questions to ask, a visit summary, and a quick "How did it go?" afterwards
• Vaccines, medicine with dose timing, growth charts with WHO percentiles, and allergies
• A clear PDF report to bring to the pediatrician

MEMORIES
• Milestones with photos
• A diary for the moments you want to remember, with weekly and monthly summaries
• Letters for later: write something for your baby to open when they're older

ALSO
• Widgets, Live Activities, Siri and an Apple Watch app
• Several babies, including twins
• A private, Face ID-locked page for your own recovery and wellbeing
• Export everything as CSV at any time

MyBaby is a tracking tool, not medical advice. Always talk to your pediatrician about your baby's health.

## Languages in App Store Connect

The app is in English, French and Spanish, so the listing is too. In App Store Connect, add these localizations
with the language menu at the top right of App Information (name, subtitle and Privacy Policy URL) and of the
version page (promotional text, description, keywords, Support URL and What's New):

- **French (Canada)** and **French** (France): both use the French text below. It's written to read naturally
  in Quebec and in France.
- **Spanish (Mexico)**: the Spanish text below, in neutral Latin American Spanish. Apple uses it for Mexico and
  most of Latin America, and for Spanish speakers in the US.
- **Spanish (Spain)**, if wanted: the same Spanish text works there too.

Screenshots fall back to the English ones until localized ones are uploaded. Each description ends with the Terms
of Use link in its own language. Text in the code blocks is pasted as is: the French uses a
non-breaking space before `:`, `;`, `?` and `!`. The counts are characters, as App Store Connect counts them.
